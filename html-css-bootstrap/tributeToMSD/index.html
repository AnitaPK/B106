MAHENDRA SINGH DHONI

Mahendra Singh Dhoni; born 7 July 1981 is an, Indian former cricketer who played as a right-handed batter and a wicket-keeper. Widely regarded as one of the most prolific wicket-keeper batsmen and captains, he represented the Indian cricket team and was the captain of the team in limited overs formats from 2007 to 2017 and in Test cricket from 2008 to 2014.

Trophies

Major International Tournaments (As Captain)

  1. ICC T20 World Cup: 2007
  2. ICC Cricket World Cup: 2011
  3. ICC Champions Trophy: 2013
  4. ACC Asia Cup: 2010 and 2016 (also part of the winning squad in 2018)

Other International Series & Achievements

  1. CB Series (Commonwealth Bank Series in Australia): 2008
  2. Border-Gavaskar Trophy: 2008–09, 2010, 2012–13 (Test series vs. Australia)
  3. ICC Test Championship Mace: 2010 and 2011 (for reaching No. 1 in Test rankings)

Franchise & Domestic Tournaments (As Captain)

  1. Indian Premier League (IPL): 5 titles with Chennai Super Kings (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, and 2023)
  2. Champions League Twenty20: 2 titles with Chennai Super Kings (2010 and 2014)

Awards

       
 National Honours (India)

Other Notable Recognitions
